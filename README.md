# eval-methodology-public

Principles, risk tiers, and qualification frameworks for gating AI work lanes prior to production — from High Capacity AI.

## What lives here
High-level grading taxonomies, pass/fail gate definitions, hallucination tolerance tiers by business task, blank scorecards, and executive essays on deterministic vs. probabilistic QA.

## What never lives here
Actual evaluation batteries, test prompts, golden datasets, grading code, model benchmarks, or internal pass-rate metrics.
